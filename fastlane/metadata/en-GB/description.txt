Ballast is a habit tracker for people a streak app has already burned.

Instead of a chain that snaps back to zero the first morning you oversleep, every habit gets one honest number: how consistently you've shown up over the last 30 days. Miss a day and the number dips a little. It never resets. There is nothing to protect and nothing to lose, so there is no reason to quit.

THE NUMBER DIPS, IT DOESN'T BREAK
• A rolling 30-day consistency percentage per habit
• A calm ring that fills as you show up — never red, never an alarm
• A 90-day dot history where gaps are just gaps, not failures
• Young habits are measured over the days they've existed, not a 30-day window they haven't earned yet

BUILT TO BE QUIET
• Today view: see your habits, tap to check in, watch the ring move
• One optional daily reminder per habit, worded gently
• A short weekly check-in from Friday to Sunday, aimed at the habit that needs it most — no judgement, just noticing
• Archive habits you're done with; their history stays

YOURS, ON YOUR PHONE
• No account, no sign-in, no subscription
• Everything stays on this device — works permanently in airplane mode
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads
• Export every check-in as CSV whenever you like

ACCESSIBLE BY DEFAULT
• Colour is never the only signal — the percentage is always shown as text
• VoiceOver labels on every habit, ring and history cell
• Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion respected throughout

Ballast is for showing up most days, not every day. Add something you want to show up for and let the number tell the truth.
