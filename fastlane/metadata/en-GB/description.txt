Ballast is the habit tracker for people a streak app has already burned. One honest number per habit — how consistently you've shown up over the last 30 days. Miss a day and it dips a little. It never resets to zero.

No account. No subscription. Nothing leaves your phone.

THE NUMBER DIPS, IT DOESN'T BREAK
Streak counters punish one bad morning by wiping out months of effort — and that's the day most people quit. Ballast measures the thing that actually matters: how often you show up. A rolling 30-day consistency percentage that moves a little every day and never collapses.

• One honest number per habit, always shown as text, never just a colour
• A calm ring that fills as you show up — never red, never an alarm
• A 90-day history where gaps are just gaps, not failures
• Young habits are measured over the days they've existed, so day three isn't "10%"

BUILT TO BE QUIET
• Today: see your habits, tap to check in, watch the ring move
• One optional daily reminder per habit, worded gently
• A short weekly check-in from Friday to Sunday, aimed at the habit that needs it most — no judgement, just noticing
• Archive habits you're done with; their history stays

YOURS, ON YOUR PHONE
• No account, no sign-in, no subscription — one price, yours for good

ACCESSIBLE BY DEFAULT
