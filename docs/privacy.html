Ballast privacy policy

Last updated 12 September 2026

Ballast is a habit tracker for iPhone. It is built so that there is nothing to collect. This page says what that means in practice.

What Ballast collects

Nothing. Ballast has no account, no sign-in, no server and no network access. The App Store privacy label for Ballast is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

The habits you create, the days you check in, and any weekly notes you write are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are included in your device backups if you have backups turned on. The developer cannot see any of it.

Reminders

If you switch on a daily reminder, Ballast asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules a local notification on the device. No push notification service is involved and nothing leaves your phone.

Export

Settings → "Export check-ins as CSV" creates a plain-text file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. Ballast contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party software development kits.

Deleting your data

Delete a habit from its detail screen to remove it and all its history. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

Ballast does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If a future version of Ballast ever adds a feature that sends data anywhere — for example optional encrypted sync — this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly described in the app.
