How the number works

Each habit shows how many of the last 30 days you checked in, as a percentage. A habit younger than 30 days is measured over the days it has existed, so a five-day-old habit with three check-ins shows 60%, not 10%. Missing a day lowers the number a little. Nothing ever resets it to zero.

Common questions

Where's my data? On your iPhone only. There is no account and no cloud. See the privacy policy.

Can I get my data out? Yes — Settings → "Export check-ins as CSV".

I checked in by mistake. Tap the circle again to undo it.

A reminder didn't arrive. Check Settings → Notifications → Ballast on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed. Ballast only asks for permission the first time you switch a reminder on.

I archived a habit — where did it go? Settings → Archived → "Bring back". Its history is kept.

What's the weekly check-in? From Friday to Sunday, Today shows a banner for a short optional note about the habit that's been hardest to keep up. It's private and you can skip it.

Can I move data to a new phone? Ballast is included in your iPhone backup, so restoring a backup brings it across.

Accessibility

Ballast supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion. Colour is never the only signal — every ring shows its percentage as text. If something doesn't work well with an assistive technology, please email; it will be fixed.
